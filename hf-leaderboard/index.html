Scoring & data

Community results cover all six tasks with three independent trials per task. Only reviewed benchmark submissions enter the community cohort; historical paper references remain separate.

Each task ranks mean scores from highest to lowest at four-decimal precision, with tied scores receiving midranks. Overall order uses the equal-weight average of six task ranks; lower is better. Equal rank sums share a competition position. Harness filters only hide rows.

The paper-reference view preserves published scores, standard deviations, relative gains and available trial counts. Community views show raw test metrics and their standard deviations; no historical baseline gains are inferred.
